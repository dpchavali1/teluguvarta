# P03 — location follows

Depends on ADR-027 (story geography), T16. Reader follows multiple places
(AP/Telangana districts, US states/cities, home town) in onboarding/Settings;
feed and alerts match story geography tags. Choice is explicit, no GPS
required. Acceptance: multi-place follow, per-place alert switch, clear
'why this story' reason, empty-state when a place has no stories, tests.

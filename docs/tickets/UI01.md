# UI01 — Compact website Home introduction

**Authority:** T14 follow-up; ADR-009/010/014. Owner authorized the interface
program on 2026-10-01. T14/T15 and design contracts are implemented; T19's
operational evidence remains open and is tracked independently.

## Goal

Bring the first story into view sooner and reduce competing promotional weight
on Home while keeping preferences reachable.

## Scope

- Replace the bordered personalization card with a secondary text link and
  concise account-free/personalized hint.
- Reduce phone edition-heading size and spacing using current tokens.
- Preserve introduction, date, feed/ranking, topics, source links, full headlines,
  and existing first-visit/returning-reader onboarding behavior.
- No API, taxonomy, source rights or mobile navigation changes.

## Acceptance

- Preference link goes to /onboarding, says Personalize your feed on first visit
  and Edit preferences after life-stage onboarding, has a 44px minimum target.
- Real rendered Home shows a lead headline above the fold on 390×844 and
  1440×900 and the short 640×400 zoom layout; no horizontal page overflow at
  320/390/768/1440 widths, light/dark, EN/TE including long headlines.
- Keyboard focus is visible and order follows the document. Axe has no
  serious/critical violations on the tested Home fixture.
- Scoped lint/typecheck/unit tests/build pass. Record browser/font and production
  evidence limitations; do not claim device or deployment validation.

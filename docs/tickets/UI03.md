# UI03 — Web feed density

T14 follow-up under accepted ADR-009/010/014. Depends on UI02.

Use complete headlines and decks in grid cards, 44px Save/source targets, and avoid stretching the lead to the full rail height. Preserve attribution, lead actions and brief Save-only contract. Verify bilingual long copy and zero/one/many fixtures.

Acceptance: scoped lint/typecheck/tests/build; browser populated/empty/error
states and light/dark EN/TE responsive checks. Record device/deployment limits.

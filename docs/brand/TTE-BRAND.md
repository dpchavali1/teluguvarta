# TTE — The Telugu Edit

## Brand decision

The public brand is **TTE**, expanded everywhere it needs context as **TTE — The Telugu Edit**.

- Canonical website: `https://tte.news`
- App/download domain: `https://tte.app`
- Product descriptor: **The Telugu world, thoughtfully edited.**
- Telugu lockup: **ది తెలుగు ఎడిట్** (use as a supporting wordmark, not as a replacement for the English name)

## Why this name

TTE is short, easy to remember, and works for Telugu people who live across the
United States, India, the United Kingdom, Canada, Australia, and elsewhere. The
expanded name explains the product immediately to English-first and second-
generation readers while retaining a clear Telugu identity.

“Edit” communicates curation, context, and judgment. It positions the product
as a calm, useful daily briefing rather than a noisy headline stream.

## Audience promise

TTE helps Telugu people understand what matters in the places they live, the
places they come from, and the communities they share. Stories are concise,
source-linked, bilingual when available, and written for real decisions about
work, study, family, money, immigration, and community life.

## Naming rules

- Use **TTE — The Telugu Edit** on first reference in formal copy.
- Use **TTE** in navigation, the app icon, compact headers, and repeat references.
- Do not use “Telugu Global” in public-facing product copy after migration.
- Keep the full name in app-store listings so people can discover the product.
- Never imply that TTE republishes source articles; describe it as original,
  source-linked summaries.

## Voice

TTE is clear, composed, curious, and welcoming. It avoids sensationalism,
insider language, unexplained acronyms, and assumptions about a reader’s
country, generation, or Telugu fluency.

Preferred line: **“The Telugu world, thoughtfully edited.”**

## Digital identity

`tte.news` is the canonical web origin. `tte.app` is reserved for app-focused
campaigns and download/deep-link flows. Both domains should redirect to HTTPS
and share the same wordmark, metadata, and social identity.

## Migration note

The internal repository/package namespace remains `teluguvarta` for now to avoid
breaking imports and deployment identifiers. Public copy, metadata, mobile
display name, and API titles migrate to TTE in this change. Existing mobile
bundle identifiers and the legacy deep-link scheme remain for install and link
continuity until a store release migration is planned.
